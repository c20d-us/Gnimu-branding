## What you may do without asking

You may use the Gnimu name and the unmodified Gnimu branding to refer to, link to, or describe the Gnimu projects. For example:

- in articles, reviews, videos, talks and social media posts about Gnimu, including ones that earn money through advertising, sponsorship or subscriptions;
- in documentation, build logs and forum posts;
- to state truthfully that your product or project works with Gnimu;
- on a circuit board made from an unmodified Gnimu board design, exactly as the design places it.

You may resize the branding. Keep its proportions and colours, and give it enough contrast with the background to stay legible.

## What you may not do without written permission

- Change the branding: redraw it, recolour it, add to it, crop it, or combine it with other marks.
- Use it on anything offered for sale, including merchandise, stickers, enclosures, kits and assembled devices, other than an unmodified board as described above.
- Use it, or a name or logo that could be confused with it, as the branding of your own project, product, company, app, domain or social media account.
- Use it in a way that suggests that Chris Halstead or the Gnimu project made, sponsors or endorses your work.
- Leave it on a modified board design. If you change a Gnimu board design, remove the Gnimu branding from the silkscreen and copper before you fabricate or distribute it. You may say in text that your design is based on Gnimu.

## Forks and modified versions

The firmware, app and hardware licences let you modify and redistribute those works. They do not give you the right to present your version as Gnimu. Give a modified version that you distribute its own name and branding, and say that it is based on Gnimu.

For any use this policy does not cover, open an issue at <https://github.com/c20d-us/Gnimu-branding/issues>.

This policy may be updated. The version in this repository is the current one.
